- **Framework/library fit.** *Resolve:* an integration spike in React/Vue/Angular/Svelte + build tools, captured in a compatibility matrix. Posts claiming "drop it into your app" are blocked until then.
- **Partner/production users.** *Resolve:* a partner shortlist + trial brief; classify any evidence honestly (interest → active evaluation → trial commitment → ship commitment → verified production deployment) and obtain consent before naming. **Never promote evidence to a later stage.**
- **User impact & review rehearsal** (`user-impact-and-review-rehearsal.md`). *Resolve:* end-user benefit/cost, device matrix, privacy/a11y/abuse. This is a rehearsal lens before formal review — label it pre-review analysis, not approval.

- **API ergonomics.** *Resolve:* reproducible friction log from clean setup through realistic integration on mobile + desktop, including failure/cleanup paths.
- **Narrative & claim accuracy.** *Resolve:* one versioned source-of-truth with a claim→evidence ledger; every deck slide and every post links to its citation.
- **Measurement.** *Resolve:* select objective-appropriate metrics with denominators and privacy boundaries (adoption ≠ impressions; a ChromeStatus counter with no reading = unknown, not zero). See Measures section.

## 6. Measures (objective-appropriate, not a single score)

For this launch, the relevant metrics (denominator + instrumentation + privacy boundary, per the measurement framework) are:
- **Adoption:** ChromeStatus counter percentage (denominator: measured page loads; limitation: counter doesn't isolate the feature; no reading = unknown, not zero); framework integration count (integration ≠ production use); verified production deployments (self-selection bias).
- **Interoperability:** other-engine implementation status (position ≠ implementation; silence ≠ opposition); Baseline availability.
- **Docs/support:** support-issue volume & themes; task-completion in usability tests.
- **Sentiment:** sentiment trend + criticism themes (volume ≠ prevalence; selection bias).
- **Team safety (only if resilience pack stands up):** incident response time; aggregate-only well-being check-ins.
- **Deliberately not a metric:** raw impressions / engagement as "success."
