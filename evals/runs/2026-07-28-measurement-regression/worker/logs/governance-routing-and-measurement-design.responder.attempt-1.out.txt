### Why not "one metric"
`[fact]` The measurement framework states: do not relabel output/interest as adoption; pair at least one leading with one lagging measure plus guardrails; record denominator, baseline, limitations, and privacy boundary for every metric. `[contradiction]` Therefore a single "proof" metric would be both unsupported and misleading. The PM's underlying need — "did this launch land?" — is met by the set below.

### Minimal metric set (fill TBC after feature/segment are known)

```yaml

name: "First successful trial integration by a representative partner/developer"
objective: "Detect early real-world fit beyond interest-rung signals"
metric: "Number of distinct representative developers/partners that complete a
         working integration of <FEATURE> in a non-toy context, with their consent to be counted"
signalType: "leading"
denominator: "Target partner/developer segment (TBC; define representative frame)"
baseline: "0 (no partner commitments today) [TBC if any prior exists]"
```
