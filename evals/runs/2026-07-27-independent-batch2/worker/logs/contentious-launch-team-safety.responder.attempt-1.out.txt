1. **Private safety check** with each affected person: contact discreetly, listen, include them in decisions that affect them. Do not send them back into the thread.
2. **Escalate the credible threat** to company security and qualified internal channels now. Whether law-enforcement is engaged depends on **[UNKNOWN]** immediacy, jurisdiction, org policy, and the targeted person's preferences where possible. **DevRel does not run this track.**

9. **Draft truthful hold/correction/narrowing/delay/rollback language** (only with engineering confirmation of what's actually true).
