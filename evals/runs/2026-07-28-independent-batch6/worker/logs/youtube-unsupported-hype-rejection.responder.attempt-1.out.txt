## 3. Challenge

### 3a. The "excitement drives views" hypothesis (the PM's premise)

- **Hypothesis (PM's):** Excitement maximizes views; therefore stronger claims are better.
- **Contradiction / counter-hypothesis:** For a developer audience, **falsifiable hype is a credibility liability, not an engagement asset.** Web developers fact-check "works in all browsers" in one line of code. Excitement that survives scrutiny (early access, "help us shape this," a real problem solved, honest constraints) generates *durable* engagement. Excitement that does not survive scrutiny generates a correction cycle.
- **Recommendation:** Keep genuine excitement; relocate it from unsupported superlatives to things that are true and motivating: early access, shaping a real API, honest scope, and a clear feedback path. This is the strategy that makes the video *more* effective, not less.

### 3b. Each overclaim, mapped to evidence rule

| Draft claim | Evidence state | Why it fails | Correction principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| "the web's most powerful AI API" | **Unknown/contradicted** superlative | Unsubstantiated, unfalsifiable, and invites instant comparison rebuttals. | State what the API *does* and the *problem* it solves. Let capability, not adjectives, carry excitement. |
| "loved by developers everywhere" | **Contradicted/unknown** | No survey; two interest-stage partners. Category interest ≠ API demand (exemplar A4). | State the real evidence: OT open, two teams *evaluating*, inviting more trial. Never promote *interest → love/commitment*. |
| "available in all browsers" | **Contradicted** (factually false) | Chrome-only, behind OT. | State exact availability (Chrome [version], OT token), feature-detection guidance, progressive enhancement. |

### 3c. Lifecycle framing (the structural problem)

- **Fact:** A "launch" video communicates stable/default availability. This feature is in origin trial.
- **Recommendation:** Re-title and re-purpose the artifact as an **"Origin Trial / experiment"** video (Phase 5 communication: invite, set expectations, collect feedback), *not* a launch video. A genuine Phase-7 launch video should wait for Phase-7 readiness. If the PM requires *a* video now, this is the only evidence-safe version of it.

### 3d. Launch-resilience lens

Overclaiming is a foreseeable-criticism trigger (Phase-7 failure mode: "Social hype omits limitations"). At minimum: one source of truth, a claims sheet traceable to evidence, prepared correction/hold language, and feedback routed to a structured issue (not personal accounts). **Recommendation:** ship the corrected script *with* the claim sheet and a feedback issue URL on-screen.
